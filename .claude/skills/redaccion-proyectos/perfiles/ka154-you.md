# Perfil: KA154-YOU - Actividades de Participación Juvenil

**Estado**: VERIFICADO. Rúbrica destilada elemento a elemento, con la sección "Cómo sacar el máximo en cada elemento", en `rubricas/ka154-you.md`.
**Última revisión**: 2026-10-01 la rúbrica y sus palancas; en 2026-08 las preguntas, contra el formulario oficial, la Guía del Programa 2026 y la guía de expertos de evaluación de calidad (ed. 2026)
**Proyectos nuestros en esta acción**: EuroÁgora (Horizonte Rioja, borrador completo en `proyectos/erasmus/`), DiáLogos (referencia aprobada)

## Qué es
Financia actividades diseñadas y ejecutadas por gente joven para participar en la vida democrática, fuera de los cauces formales. Puede solicitarla un grupo informal de jóvenes o una organización. Admite proyectos nacionales o transnacionales, con actividades presenciales, online o mixtas. Es la acción donde más pesa que los jóvenes decidan, no solo asistan.

## Datos de la convocatoria
- Agencia: nacional (en España, INJUVE ES02)
- Duración elegible: 3 a 24 meses
- Idioma de presentación: cualquiera oficial de la UE. Redactamos en español con traducción al inglés donde el formulario la pide (resumen).
- Reconocimiento: Youthpass

## Bloques del formulario
Siete bloques, un redactor por bloque.

1. **resumen** - Project Summary. Cada respuesta en español y su traducción al inglés, 900 a 1.500 caracteres por idioma.
   - "What do you want to achieve by implementing the project? What are the objectives of your project?"
   - "What activities do you plan to implement? What is the number and profile of the participants involved?"
   - "What results and impact do you expect your project to have?"
2. **solicitante** - Applicant background. Nada de OID, dirección exacta ni aclarar que no es un organismo público: la aplicación ya lo muestra. El campo se gasta en actividad real, volumen de gente y expertise del equipo.
   - "Background and experience. Please briefly present your organisation (e.g. its type, scope of work, areas of activity and if applicable, approximate number of paid/unpaid staff, learners and members of the group)."
   - "What are the activities and experience of the organisation in the areas relevant for this project? What are the skills and/or expertise of key persons involved in this project?"
   - Más una plantilla breve de background por socia, con placeholders.
3. **relevancia** - Project Rationale. Concentra los 30 puntos de relevancia.
   - "Aims. Please describe the idea of your project including the needs you have identified [...] and the project's objectives."
   - "Does your project address one or more of the priorities defined in the context of the EU Youth Dialogue or the European Youth Goals? If yes, please explain how."
   - "How does your project link to the objectives of the Erasmus programme and more specifically those of Youth Participation Activities?"
   - "Target Group. What are the target groups of the individual activities of your project?"
   - "Expected impact. How will your project benefit the organisation(s) and the young people actively involved in the preparation and implementation of the project, during and after the project lifetime?"
   - "How will your project benefit young people in the wider target group during and after the project lifetime?"
   - "What would be the impact of your project at local, regional, national and/or European level?"
   - "Topic. Please select up to three topics addressed by your project."
4. **actividades** - Description of the activities.
   - "Please list in chronological order all the different activities that you will carry out in your project." Mes a mes, siguiendo la sección 5 de la biblia. 4.000 a 6.000 caracteres.
   - "Please describe the profile of the target groups you will address by the different activities, including their age. [...] please describe how they have been, or will be selected."
   - "Please describe how the target groups will be involved in planning, preparing, and implementing the activities and in the follow-up of the different activities."
   - "Please provide an estimate of the number of young people that will not take part in physical events or mobility activities, but still will participate in other activities of your project." Y su "Out of which No. of Participants with Fewer Opportunities".
   - "Will your project also involve decision makers?" Y "Please provide a description of who they are, why you plan to involve them, and what their role will be."
   - "How will the participants cooperate and communicate between them to prepare and follow-up on the project activities? How did you choose this channel/these channels of communication?"
   - "Which learning outcomes or competences (i.e. knowledge, skills and attitudes/behaviours) are to be acquired/improved by the participants in the project activities?"
5. **eventos** - Project Details, una ficha por actividad presencial. Campos: Activity Type, Country of the event, City of Venue, Duration of the event (in days), Start y End date, Number of participants in event, Out of which with Fewer Opportunities, No. of Facilitators, Total persons, Green travel, Participation fees. Debajo, el programa día a día en prosa y el reparto de quién organiza qué.
6. **diseno** - Project Design. Concentra los 40 puntos de calidad del diseño.
   - "Non-formal learning. What will the participants learn during the activities? [...] What non-formal learning methods will you use in your project?"
   - "Preparation, support and follow-up. How will you prepare the participants before the start of the activities and how will you support them during and after the activities?"
   - "What measures will you put in place to ensure the safety and protection of participants?"
   - "Recognition of learning outcomes. How will you help participants to become aware of what they have learned and which competences they have developed or improved?" Más "Will your project make use of such European instruments/certificates?" y "Are you planning to use any national instrument/certificate?"
   - "Participants with fewer opportunities. Are you planning to involve participants facing situations that make their participation in the activities more difficult?"
   - "Virtual learning/Blended activities. Do you foresee Virtual/Blended activities and/or the use of any virtual component, before, during or after the activity?" Más el porcentaje estimado de participantes que usarán componentes virtuales.
   - "Environmental friendly practices. Will you include sustainable and environmental-friendly practices in your activities?"
7. **gestion** - Project Management. Concentra los 30 puntos de gestión.
   - "How will you manage the project (agreements with partners etc.) and make sure that it is done in line with the Erasmus Youth Quality Standards?"
   - "How will you organise the practical and logistical part of the project (e.g. ongoing activities, communication with participants and partners, if needed mentoring and support, and if relevant travel, accommodation, insurance, visa, social security, etc.)?"
   - "Partnerships. How and why did you choose your project partners? What experiences and competences will they bring to the project?"
   - "How will you communicate with them?"
   - "How will you monitor and coordinate their contribution?"
   - "Which other actors (organisations or individuals) will be involved and how?"
   - "Evaluation. How will you evaluate your project's success? Which activities will you carry out in order to assess whether, and to what extent, your project has reached its objectives and results?"
   - "Sustainability of the results. What will you do to make sure that your project continues to have effects also after it ends?"
   - "Dissemination of project results. How will you make your project visible outside your organisation(s)? What concrete results of the project do you plan to disseminate, how and to whom?"
   - "How will you involve participants in such activities?"

## Criterios de adjudicación
| Criterio | Puntos |
|---|---|
| Relevancia, justificación e impacto | 30 |
| Calidad del diseño y la implementación | 40 |
| Calidad de la gestión | 30 |

Umbral: 60 sobre 100 en total y al menos la mitad de cada criterio.

## Rúbrica destilada
Elemento a elemento, con puntos, umbral, bandas y palancas para sacar el máximo, en `rubricas/ka154-you.md`. Los verificadores de rúbrica trabajan con ese fichero. Lo propio de la acción que más pesa: jóvenes con papel significativo en todas las fases desde la concepción, qué forma de participación nueva o alternativa pilota el proyecto, valor añadido europeo también en proyectos nacionales y, sin socias, cómo se reparten las tareas dentro de la organización y qué papel tiene el coach si lo hay.

## Trampas propias de esta acción
- Los campos numéricos (participantes con menos oportunidades, participantes solo online) nunca se dejan sin cifra sugerida.
- El viaje sostenible se declara por flujo, no por actividad. Canarias y Baleares vuelan y ese flujo se declara sin viaje sostenible por inexistencia de alternativa razonable.
- La Tarjeta Sanitaria Europea es irrelevante en movilidad nacional y delata copia de otra solicitud. En España, Sistema Nacional de Salud.
- Los códigos internos N1 y O1 solo valen donde se enumeran y etiquetan las necesidades y los objetivos.
- Aritmética interna: número de ponencias contra número de temáticas, plazas por socia contra total, personas igual a participantes más facilitadores. El evaluador cuenta.
- El papel de la gente joven en la concepción es el corazón de la acción. Un comité organizador joven con funciones y calendario, no una frase.
