STATUTES

ASOCIACIÓN CULTURAL "Estrellas del Sur"

Chapter I · General provisions

Article 1. Name and nature

Under the name ASOCIACIÓN CULTURAL "Estrellas del Sur" (Cultural Association "Estrellas del Sur"), an association-based, non-profit organisation is hereby constituted in Córdoba (province) on 30 August 2020, under Article 22 of the Spanish Constitution, Organic Law 1/2002 of 22 March, regulating the Right of Association, and the other provisions in force issued in development and application thereof, Law 4/2006 of 23 June on Associations of Andalusia, and the corresponding concordant legal provisions. The regime of the Association shall be determined by the provisions of these Statutes.

Article 2. Legal personality and capacity

The Association hereby constituted has its own legal personality and full capacity to act, and may consequently perform all such acts as may be necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was created, subject to the provisions of the legal system.

Article 3. Nationality and registered office

The Association hereby created has Spanish nationality. The registered office of the Association shall be located at Avenida Guerrita 14, 1st floor, unit 3A, Córdoba (Córdoba), postal code 14005. Any change of registered office shall require a resolution of the General Assembly, specifically convened for that purpose, and the amendment of these Statutes. The resolution of the General Assembly must be notified to the Register of Associations within one month and shall take effect, both for members and for third parties, only once registration has taken place.

Article 4. Territorial scope

The territorial scope within which the association will mainly carry out its activities is local, provincial and regional.

Article 5. Duration

The Association is constituted for an indefinite period.

Chapter II · Purpose of the association

Article 6. Aims

The aims of the Association shall be the following:

  1. To develop and promote local cultural activity.
  2. To promote interculturality and European diversity.
  3. To encourage international cooperation activities for civic and social development.
  4. To promote equal opportunities between men and women.
  5. To promote the participation and presence of women in political, economic, cultural and social life.
  6. To promote the effective and comprehensive integration of vulnerable groups into society.
  7. To empower young people to take an active part in economic, socio-cultural and political life.
  8. To act as agents of change in order to contribute to social and ecological justice.

And, in order to achieve them, the following activities shall be carried out:

  1. Carrying out internationalisation and cultural promotion projects in our region and beyond.
  2. Participating in and developing youth projects under the Erasmus+ programme calls.
  3. Participating in volunteering programmes of the European Solidarity Corps and supporting the local volunteering network.
  4. Developing and promoting local programmes that foster social well-being.
  5. Supporting and accompanying the most vulnerable groups in our region.
  6. Evaluating strategies and adapting projects in order to tackle the social challenges of the current socio-cultural context.
  7. Contributing to the achievement of sustainable objectives proactively and comprehensively in all aspects of our work.
  8. Including vulnerable groups in the creation, development, delivery and evaluation of the entity's projects.
  9. Creating and disseminating cultural content on a web platform.

Chapter III · General Assembly

Article 7. General Assembly

The supreme governing body of the Association is the General Assembly (Asamblea General), made up of all the members. It adopts its resolutions by the majority principle, or internal democracy, and must meet at least once a year. Assemblies may be ordinary or extraordinary and shall be convened in the manner set out in these Statutes.

Article 8. Convening

Assemblies shall be convened by the person holding the office of Chair (Presidencia), on their own initiative, by resolution of the Governing Board (Junta Directiva), or at the request of a number of members not lower than 10 per cent.

Once the Governing Board has resolved to convene a General Assembly, the Chair shall convene it within a maximum of fifteen calendar days from the adoption of the resolution. At least fifteen calendar days must elapse between the notice of the meeting and the date set for the Assembly to be held.

A request to convene made by the members shall contain the agenda of the session and shall enclose such documents or information as may be necessary for the adoption of the resolutions, provided that such documentation or information must be taken into account for that purpose. The request must be submitted to the Secretary's office of the Association; once stamped, a copy shall be returned to the applicant. The person holding the office of Secretary (Secretaría) of the Association, after verifying the formal requirements (number of members, notice and documentation, where applicable), shall immediately inform the Chair so that, within fifteen calendar days from its submission, the Chair convenes the Assembly, which must be held within thirty calendar days from the date of the request. If the request does not meet the formal requirements, the Secretary shall treat it as not having been made and shall file it, notifying the member heading the list or the signatures.

If the Chair does not convene the Assembly within the periods set out in the preceding paragraph, the promoters shall be entitled to convene the General Assembly themselves, stating those circumstances in the notice, which shall be signed by the person heading the signatures or the list of the request.

Article 9. Form of the notice

The notice given by the persons entitled to do so, in accordance with the preceding Article, must be communicated and displayed on the notice board, if there is one, fifteen calendar days before the Assembly is held. The notice must contain the agenda, as well as the place, date and time of the meeting on first and second call. The necessary documentation and information to be taken into account for the adoption of the resolutions shall be available to the members at the Secretary's office of the Association at least fifteen calendar days before the Assembly is held.

Article 10. Ordinary General Assembly

The Ordinary General Assembly shall be held at least once a year, within the four months following the close of the financial year, in order to deal with the following items on the agenda:

  1. Reading and approval, where appropriate, of the minutes of the previous session (whether an ordinary or an extraordinary General Assembly).
  2. Examination and approval, where appropriate, of the accounts for the previous financial year.
  3. Examination and approval, where appropriate, of the budget for the financial year.
  4. Examination of the report of activities and approval, where appropriate, of the management of the Governing Board.
  5. Approval, where appropriate, of the programme of activities.

Article 11. Extraordinary General Assembly

The adoption of any resolution other than those mentioned in the preceding Article shall require an Extraordinary General Assembly to be convened, and specifically in order to deal with the following:

  1. Amendment of the Statutes.
  2. Dissolution of the Association.
  3. Appointment of the Governing Board.
  4. Disposal and transfer of assets.
  5. Constitution of a Federation, Confederation or Union of Associations, or joining one where it already exists.
  6. Approval of a change of registered office, which shall entail the amendment of the Statutes.

Article 12. Quorum

The General Assembly, whether ordinary or extraordinary, shall be validly constituted on first call when at least one third of the members are present or represented; on second call, whatever the number of members attending. For the purpose of counting members or the total number of votes, the proxies or delegations granted by the members shall be presented to the Secretary at the beginning of the session. The persons who are to act as Chair and Secretary of the Assembly shall be designated at the beginning of the meeting.

Article 13. Adoption of resolutions

All matters shall be debated and voted on in the order in which they appear on the agenda. The person acting as Chair shall open the debate, opening a first round of interventions in which the floor shall be taken subject to prior authorisation; the Chair shall also moderate the debates and may open a second round of interventions or grant the floor in response to references made.

Resolutions of the General Assembly shall be adopted by a simple majority of the persons present or represented, where the votes in favour exceed the votes against. However, a qualified majority of 60%, which shall result where the votes in favour exceed half of those cast by the members present or represented, shall be required for resolutions concerning the dissolution of the association, the amendment of the Statutes, the disposal or transfer of assets, and the remuneration of the members of the Governing Board.

Resolutions of the General Assembly affecting the name of the Association, its registered office, its statutory aims and activities, its territorial scope and other amendments to the Statutes, as well as those concerning the designation of the members of the Governing Board, the opening and closing of branches, the constitution of federations, confederations and unions, and dissolution, shall be notified to the Register of Associations for registration within one month from the adoption of the resolution.

Article 14. Proxies and delegation of votes

A proxy or delegation of vote shall be valid only for the session or the notice for which it is issued, and any indefinite delegation or proxy shall be null and void. The proxy or delegation of vote shall be recorded in writing, stating the personal details and membership number of the delegating and the represented person, and signed and initialled by both.

Chapter IV · Governing Board

Article 15. Definition and term of office

The Governing Board (Junta Directiva) is the collegiate body that manages, administers and represents the interests of the association, without prejudice to the powers of the General Assembly as the sovereign body. Its term of office shall last 4 years; once that period has elapsed, it shall be renewed and the renewal registered accordingly. The General Assembly for the appointment or renewal of the Governing Board must be convened before the end of the established term of office. Members of the Governing Board who have exhausted the term for which they were elected shall continue to hold their positions, in an acting capacity, until the persons replacing them accept office. The Governing Board may bring elections forward, shortening its own term of office if so established in a session of the Governing Board, giving notice to the General Assembly at least 6 months before the scheduled elections.

Article 16. Composition and offices

The Governing Board shall be made up of a minimum of four members; among them, the offices of Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary and Treasurer shall be designated on a mandatory basis. All members shall be appointed and removed by the General Assembly. Office shall be held in person, and votes may not be delegated at sessions of the Governing Board.

Article 17. Election

In order to be part of the Governing Board it shall be an essential requirement to be of legal age, to be in full enjoyment of civil rights and not to be affected by the grounds of incompatibility established in the legislation in force. The members of the Governing Board shall be elected from among the members, at an Extraordinary General Assembly, in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of the Statutes. Once a General Assembly has been convened for the designation of the Governing Board, members wishing to exercise their right to be elected must submit their candidacy at least twenty-four hours before the Assembly is held. Where a vacancy arises, the Governing Board may designate another person who is part of it to fill it provisionally, until the definitive election is held by the General Assembly convened for that purpose.

Article 18. Termination of office

1. The members of the Governing Board shall cease to hold their respective offices on the following grounds:

  1. Death or declaration of death.
  2. Incapacity, disqualification or incompatibility, in accordance with the legal system.
  3. Judicial decision.
  4. Expiry of their term of office. However, until the General Assembly elects the new Governing Board, the outgoing Board shall continue in an acting capacity, and that status must be stated in any documents they must sign by virtue of their respective offices.
  5. Voluntary resignation, notified in writing to the Governing Board.
  6. A resolution adopted by the General Assembly with the statutory formalities.
  7. Loss of membership.

2. Terminations of office and appointments shall be notified to the Register of Associations for due record and publicity.

Article 19. The Chair

The person holding the office of Chair shall be responsible for:

  1. Legally representing the Association before all kinds of persons, authorities and public or private entities.
  2. Convening the meetings of the Governing Board and of the General Assembly, chairing them, conducting their debates, and suspending and closing the sessions.
  3. Implementing the resolutions of the Governing Board and of the General Assembly, being able for that purpose to perform all kinds of acts and contracts and to sign the documents necessary to that end, without prejudice to the fact that each body, in the exercise of its powers, may, when adopting resolutions, expressly empower any other member of the Governing Board to implement them.
  4. Complying with and ensuring compliance with the resolutions of the Governing Board and the General Assembly.
  5. Ordering payments and authorising expenditure.
  6. Casting the deciding vote in the event of a tie.
  7. Endorsing the minutes and certifications of the resolutions of the Governing Board and the General Assembly.
  8. Adopting any urgent measure that the good running of the Association makes advisable, or that proves necessary or appropriate in the performance of their duties, without prejudice to subsequently reporting to the Governing Board.
  9. Exercising such other functions as are inherent to their status as Chair of the Governing Board and of the Association.

Article 20. The Vice-Chair

The person holding the office of Vice-Chair (Vicepresidencia) shall perform the functions of the Chair where the office is vacant by reason of absence or illness, and may also act on behalf of the Association in those cases in which this is so resolved by the Governing Board or the General Assembly.

Article 21. The Secretary

The person holding the office of Secretary shall be responsible for the following functions:

  1. Attending the sessions of the Governing Board and of the Assembly and drafting and authorising their minutes.
  2. Issuing the notices for the sessions of the Governing Board and of the Assembly.
  3. Immediately informing the Chair of any request to convene made by the members in the manner provided for in Article 8 of these Statutes.
  4. Receiving and processing the communications of the members of the Governing Board and of the members, as well as notifications, requests for data, corrections, certifications or any other kind of written documents of which the Secretary must be aware.
  5. Processing the registrable corporate resolutions with the corresponding Registers.
  6. Preparing the handling of business, as well as the documentation to be used or taken into account.
  7. Issuing certifications of the resolutions approved and any other certifications, with the approval of the Chair, as well as such reports as may be necessary.
  8. Holding under their responsibility and custody the files, documents and books of the Association, with the exception of the accounting books.
  9. Any other functions inherent to the Secretary's office.

In cases of absence or illness and, in general, where any justified cause arises, the person holding the office of Secretary shall be replaced by the youngest ordinary board member.

Article 22. The Treasurer

The person holding the office of Treasurer (Tesorería) shall be responsible for:

  1. Collecting the funds of the Association, holding them in custody and investing them in the manner determined by the Governing Board.
  2. Making payments, with the approval of the Chair.
  3. Countersigning all receipt and payment documents, with the approval of the Chair.
  4. Keeping the accounting books and complying with the tax obligations of the Association in due time and form.
  5. Preparing the draft budget for approval by the Governing Board and subsequent submission to the General Assembly. The same procedure shall be followed in respect of the General Statement of Accounts for its annual approval by the Assembly.
  6. Any others inherent to their status as Treasurer, as the person responsible for economic and financial management.

Article 23. Ordinary board members

The ordinary board members (vocales) shall have the obligations proper to their office as members of the Governing Board, as well as those entrusted to them by the Board itself through the creation of delegations or working committees.

Article 24. Powers of attorney

The Governing Board may appoint general or special attorneys.

Article 25. Notices and sessions

1. For the Governing Board to be validly constituted for the purposes of holding sessions, deliberating and adopting resolutions, half of its members must be present, the presence of the Chair and the Secretary, or of those replacing them, being necessarily required.

2. The Governing Board shall meet at least once a quarter and as many times as necessary for the good running of the Association, upon notice given by the Chair, on their own initiative or at the initiative of any of its members.

3. The notice of its meetings shall be issued with the formal requirements (agenda, place and date) and shall be delivered at least 48 hours before the meeting is held.

4. Deliberations shall follow the same regime as set out in Article 13 for the General Assembly. Resolutions shall be adopted by a simple majority of the votes cast, with the Chair casting the deciding vote in the event of a tie.

5. No resolution may be adopted that does not appear on the agenda, unless, with all the members making up the Governing Board present, they so agree unanimously.

6. The Governing Board shall likewise be validly constituted without prior notice where, with all its members present, this is so agreed unanimously, the provisions of the preceding paragraph applying as regards resolutions. Boards constituted in this way shall be called a Universal Governing Board.

7. Sessions of the Governing Board may be attended, with the right to speak but not to vote, by those persons previously summoned or invited by the Chair, whose functions shall be exclusively advisory.

Article 26. Powers

The Governing Board shall have the following powers:

  1. To draw up the Plan of Activities.
  2. To grant general or special powers of attorney.
  3. To organise and carry out the activities approved by the General Assembly.
  4. To approve the Draft Budget for its definitive approval by the General Assembly.
  5. To approve the statement of accounts prepared by the Treasurer, for its definitive approval, where appropriate, by the General Assembly.
  6. To draw up the annual report of activities to be reported to the General Assembly.
  7. To create such Working Committees as it deems appropriate for the development of the functions entrusted and the activities approved, as well as for any other matters arising from the fulfilment of the corporate aims. Such committees shall regulate their internal functioning in the manner agreed by them at their first constitutive session.
  8. To decide on applications relating to the admission of members.

Article 27. Obligations and liability of the members of the Governing Board

The obligations of the members of the Governing Board include, by way of illustration, complying with and ensuring compliance with the aims of the Association, attending the meetings to which they are convened, discharging their office with the due diligence of a loyal representative, and complying in their actions with the provisions of the legal rules in force and of these Statutes. The members of the Governing Board shall be liable to the Association for any damages caused by acts contrary to the Law or to the Statutes, or by acts performed negligently. Those who expressly opposed the resolution giving rise to such acts, or who did not take part in its adoption, shall be exempt from liability.

Article 28. Unpaid nature of office

The members of the Governing Board shall discharge their office free of charge, and may under no circumstances receive remuneration for the performance of their function, without prejudice to the right to be reimbursed for the expenses incurred in the exercise of their offices, provided that these are duly and formally justified.

Chapter V · Youth Section

Article 29. Definition and Governing Board of the Youth Section

The Youth Section (Sección Juvenil) is a section of the association itself with a Governing Board made up of at least four members aged between 18 and 30, holding the same offices as the Governing Board of the association. All members of the association aged between 14 and 30 shall form part of this section. It proposes activities by and for young people with the main aim of fostering youth participation in various spaces at regional, provincial and local level.

Article 30. Term of office and offices of the Governing Board of the Youth Section

Its term of office shall last 4 years; once that period has elapsed, it shall be renewed and the renewal registered accordingly. The General Assembly for the appointment or renewal of the Governing Board of the Youth Section must be convened before the end of the established term of office. Members who have exhausted the term for which they were elected shall continue to hold their positions, in an acting capacity, until the persons replacing them accept office. The Governing Board of the Youth Section shall be made up of a minimum of four members; among them, the offices of Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary and Treasurer shall be designated. All members shall be appointed and removed by the General Assembly. Office shall be held in person, and votes may not be delegated at sessions. The election and termination of office of a person on the Governing Board of the Youth Section shall be carried out as provided for in Articles 17 and 18 respectively. The office holders shall perform the same functions as their counterparts in the offices of the association, as established in Articles 19, 20, 21, 22 and 23 of these Statutes. The Governing Board of the Youth Section may bring elections forward, shortening its own term of office if so established in a session of the Governing Board of the Youth Section, giving notice to the General Assembly of the association at least 6 months before the scheduled elections.

Chapter VI · Common provisions for the governing bodies

Article 31. Minutes

1. Minutes shall be taken by the person holding the office of Secretary of every session held by the General Assembly and the Governing Board, necessarily specifying the quorum reached for valid constitution, the agenda of the meeting, the circumstances of place and time in which it was held, the main points of the deliberations, as well as the content of the resolutions adopted; the minutes of the representative body shall necessarily state those attending.

2. At the request of the members, the minutes shall state, where applicable, their vote against the resolution adopted, their abstention and the reasons justifying it, or the sense of their vote in favour. Likewise, any member has the right to request the full transcription of their intervention or proposal, provided that they submit, at the time or within forty-eight hours, the text faithfully corresponding to their intervention, which shall be recorded in the minutes or attached thereto as a copy.

3. The minutes shall be approved at the same or at the following session; notwithstanding the foregoing, the person holding the office of Secretary may issue certifications of the specific resolutions that have been adopted, expressly stating that the minutes are pending subsequent approval.

4. The minutes shall be signed by the person holding the office of Secretary and endorsed by the Chair; all minutes of meetings of the governing and representative bodies must be recorded in the corresponding book of minutes.

Article 32. Challenge of resolutions

Resolutions of the General Assembly and of the Governing Board may be challenged before the civil courts in the manner legally established. Members may challenge the resolutions and actions of the association that they consider contrary to the Statutes within forty days from the date of their adoption, seeking their correction or annulment and, where appropriate, their preventive suspension, or combining both claims through the procedures established in the Civil Procedure Act. Until the internal disputes that may arise in the association are resolved, applications for entry in the register concerning the disputed matters shall give rise only to provisional annotations.

Chapter VII · Members

Article 33. Classes

1. The following classes of members shall exist within the Association:

  1. Founding members, being those who take part in the act of constitution of the Association.
  2. Ordinary members, being those who join after the constitution of the Association.
  3. Honorary members, being those who, by reason of their prestige or for having contributed significantly to the dignity and development of the association, become deserving of such distinction. The appointment of honorary members shall be a matter for the Governing Board.

2. The association shall keep an updated list of its members.

Article 34. Acquisition of membership

In order to acquire membership it is necessary to be a natural or legal person and to be interested in the development of the aims of the Association. Natural persons must be of legal age or emancipated minors with full capacity to act, and must not be subject to any legal condition affecting the exercise of the right. Non-emancipated minors over fourteen years of age require the documented consent of the persons who must supplement their capacity. Legal persons of an associative nature shall require the express resolution of their competent body, and those of an institutional nature, the resolution of their governing body. An application to acquire membership shall be accepted by the Governing Board, provided that the requirements laid down in the Statutes are met.

Article 35. Loss of membership

Membership shall be lost on any of the following grounds:

  1. By the member's own free will. For this purpose, it shall suffice to submit a written resignation addressed to the Governing Board and filed with the Secretary's office of the Association. It shall take effect immediately, from the date of its submission.
  2. By serious breach of these Statutes or of the resolutions validly adopted by the corporate bodies.
  3. By death or declaration of death.
  4. By non-payment of the association's fees.
  5. By total inactivity in the life and activities proposed by the association for a period exceeding 3 months. Members may recover their membership by returning to participation in the activity of the association.

For membership to be lost on this ground, a reasoned resolution of the Governing Board, adopted by 2/3 of the number of votes lawfully cast, shall be an essential requirement. Every member shall have the right to be informed of the facts giving rise to expulsion and to be heard before such a resolution is adopted. In the event of a sanction of separation of the member, ratification by the General Assembly shall be required in all cases.

Article 36. Rights

1. The rights of ordinary and founding members are:

  1. To take part in the Assemblies with the right to speak and to vote.
  2. To be electors and eligible for office.
  3. To be informed about the composition of the governing and representative bodies of the association, its statement of accounts and the development of its activity.
  4. To be heard before disciplinary measures are adopted against them and to be informed of the facts giving rise to such measures, the resolution imposing the sanction, where applicable, having to be reasoned.
  5. To access the documentation of the association through the Governing Board. To take part in the activities of the association and to use the goods and facilities for common use of the Association, with respect for the equal right of the other members.

2. Honorary members shall have the same rights as founding and ordinary members with the exception of those provided for in paragraphs a), b) and g). Notwithstanding the foregoing, they may attend General Assemblies with the right to speak but not to vote.

Article 37. Obligations

The duties of founding and ordinary members are:

  1. To share the purposes of the association and to collaborate in achieving them.
  2. To pay the special levies, fees and other contributions which, in accordance with the Statutes, may correspond to each member.
  3. To comply with the remaining obligations arising from the statutory provisions.
  4. To abide by and comply with the resolutions validly adopted by the Governing Board and the General Assembly.
  5. To participate in, support, influence or contribute to the development of the activity of the association on a regular basis.

Without prejudice to the loss of membership for non-payment of membership fees, and while expulsion is being processed, the member shall have their active and passive voting rights suspended. Such suspension of rights shall take place upon the non-payment of a single fee and shall last until it is regularised or until membership is definitively lost.

Chapter VIII · Economic regime

Article 38. Founding assets

The founding or initial assets of the Association at the time of its constitution amount to 4 euros.

Article 39. Ownership of goods and rights

The Association must appear as the owner of all the goods and rights making up its assets, which shall be recorded in its inventory and registered, where appropriate, in the corresponding Public Registers.

Article 40. Economic resources

For the development of its activities, the Association shall be financed by:

  1. The resources deriving from the yield of its assets, where applicable.
  2. The membership fees, ordinary or extraordinary.
  3. The donations or grants that may be awarded by natural or legal persons, public or private.
  4. Donations, inheritances or legacies, accepted by the Governing Board.
  5. The income deriving from its activities.

The profits obtained by the Association from carrying out economic activities, including the provision of services, must be allocated exclusively to the fulfilment of its aims, and may under no circumstances be distributed among the members or among their spouses or persons living with them in an analogous relationship of affection, or among their relatives, nor transferred free of charge to natural or legal persons with a profit-making interest.

Article 41. Financial year, budget and accounting

1. The financial year shall coincide with the calendar year; it shall begin on 1 January and shall close on 31 December each year.

2. The Governing Board shall draw up the Budget annually and it shall be approved by the General Assembly. With the approval of the Budget, the ordinary fees for the corresponding financial year shall be deemed approved. For the approval of extraordinary fees, an Extraordinary General Assembly must be convened, unless the Association lacks liquidity and the corresponding disposal and expenditure are urgent, in which case it shall suffice for the resolution to be adopted by the Governing Board, following a report by the Treasurer and subsequent ratification by the General Assembly, which must be approved within thirty days following the adoption of the resolution by the Governing Board.

3. The General Assembly shall annually approve the accounts of the Association, once the budgetary year to which they correspond has ended.

4. The Governing Board must keep accounts that allow a true and fair view to be obtained of the assets, the results and the financial situation of the entity.

Chapter IX · Dissolution and application of corporate assets

Article 42. Dissolution

The Association shall be dissolved on the following grounds:

  1. By a resolution adopted by a qualified majority of 60% at an Extraordinary General Assembly.
  2. On the grounds determined in Article 39 of the Civil Code.
  3. By a final judicial decision.
  4. On the grounds determined in these Statutes.

Article 43. Liquidation

1. Once the dissolution of the Association has been resolved, the liquidation period opens, until the end of which the entity shall retain its legal personality.

2. The members of the Governing Board at the time of dissolution shall become liquidators, unless they are expressly designated by the General Assembly or by the judge who, where applicable, orders the dissolution. It shall be for the liquidators:

  1. To safeguard the integrity of the assets of the Association.
  2. To conclude the pending operations and to carry out the new ones necessary for the liquidation.
  3. To collect the credits of the Association.
  4. To liquidate the assets and pay the creditors.
  5. To apply the remaining assets to the purposes provided for in the Statutes.
  6. To request the cancellation of the entries in the Register.

3. The net surplus resulting from the liquidation shall be delivered directly to a public or private non-profit entity with aims similar to those of the Association, which has distinguished itself in the exercise of its activities.

4. In the event of insolvency of the Association, the Governing Board or, where applicable, the liquidators, must immediately initiate the appropriate insolvency proceedings before the competent court.

Additional provision

In all matters not provided for in these Statutes, Organic Law 1/2002 of 22 March, regulating the Right of Association, Law 4/2006 of 23 June on Associations of Andalusia, and other supplementary provisions shall apply.
